# The playbook in one picture

<p class="lead">Everything this playbook covers, in four layers.</p>

## In one picture

[![The playbook in four layers: architecture, delivery loop, AI, and foundation](../assets/memos/playbook.svg)](../assets/memos/playbook.svg)

## Remember

- **Architecture** is what the app is made of: seven parts, and only two of them hold data.
- **The delivery loop** is how every change reaches users: the same eight steps, every time.
- **AI has two jobs:** answering questions inside the app, and helping you build the app.
- **The foundation** sits under everything: infrastructure in code, no passwords, backups, budgets.
- **Start small.** Add a part only when you can say why you need it.

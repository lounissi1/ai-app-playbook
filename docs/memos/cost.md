# Cost guardrails

<p class="lead">What bills, what's easy to miss, and six brakes to keep the bill small.</p>

## In one picture

[![Three columns, bills while it runs, bills when used, easy to miss; then six brakes in order; a budget alerts but does not stop spending](../assets/memos/cost.svg)](../assets/memos/cost.svg)

## Remember

- **Most services bill while they run, not while they're used.**
- **Create the budget first,** before any other resource.
- **Scale to zero** wherever a slow first request is acceptable.
- **Tag everything** so you can see who and what costs money.
- **A budget only alerts.** Tear down what you no longer need.
